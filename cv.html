Enterprise Agent Platform — agent governance 2026

Personal Proof of concept

Problem
An agent can be given capability long before an enterprise can decide who may authorise it, what it may touch, and how anyone proves afterwards what it did.
Constraint
Workflow engines and AI copilots assume either that a human is present at every step, or that nothing needs approving. Neither survives contact with a regulated process.
My role
Designer and builder — the process model, the governance model, and the working prototype.
Mechanisms
Named-signer mandate with delegation and separation of duties; six fail-closed gates bound to version and parameter digest; a dual ledger (business facts plus hash-chained attestations) with reconciliation; approve, stop and audit as first-class operations.
Maturity
Proof of concept, built in two weeks, with real review behind it. Zero scenarios released to production — at capture this was the build, not the release, and that distinction is exactly what the design exists to make visible.
Validation
Discussed and tested with APAC process owners and IT business partners, then presented to the regional IT VP and to the global AI & Data VP as a prototype of what AI transformation could look like. That executive review is why the maturity label is stated rather than implied.
Verification
475 processes · 8-step delivery journey · 6 gates · dual ledger. 26 business scenarios across 6 agent workstreams; 191 EPM processes, 2,073 BPMN agent cells, 103 human roles.
Agentic Business Process Management — end-to-end process redesign 2026

Personal Proof of concept

Problem
AI adoption stalls at the task level because an enterprise governs processes, not prompts. The unit that needs redesign is the end-to-end process, with agents inside it.
My role
Redesigned five end-to-end process families as agent-hosted flows: Source-to-Settle, Plan-to-Make, Make-to-Stock, Order-to-Cash and Finance.
Mechanisms
Agent-to-process attachment points, human approval points, agent registry and activation, knowledge and governance modules — so an agent is an accountable process participant rather than a plugin.
Maturity
Proof of concept; prototype interfaces only.
Verification
26 scenarios, 6 workstreams and 103 human roles modelled; captured in the platform screenshots on the project page.
