1. PMO Platform for a Global SAP S/4HANA Rollout
Maturity: Enterprise delivery
Period: 2025 - 2026
Role: PMO, inside the programme

Problem: A multi-country, multi-workstream SAP implementation was governed through spreadsheets
and meetings, with no single source of truth for plan, risk, resources, budget or deliverables.
Constraint: A commercial PPM suite was already in place, but it did not carry this programme's
category dictionary, WBS/SOW chain or escalation logic, and could not be changed at the speed
the programme needed.
My role: Programme PMO. I owned the process model, definitions, data design, trade-offs and
acceptance. Built with AI-assisted development; the code stays with the programme.
Mechanisms designed: Programme - Workstream - SOW - WBS - Deliverable - RAID - Escalation -
tracker data chain; a 455-item category dictionary (L1 6, L2 61, L3 388) as the shared
vocabulary.
Adoption: Used by the global programme PMO and as the vehicle for APAC process-owner readiness
training across ten countries. The platform aligned PMO practice before rollout rather than only
reporting on it; the category dictionary and data model carried the global template design and
the localisation practice.
Verification: 56 business pages, 51 data tables, 119,000 lines of code, 1,485 processes and
4,759 process facts, 4 programmes, 53 WBS items, 24 deliverables, 13 RAID entries (4 escalated),
48 SOW lines, 44 resources, 25 workshops; zero to live in 4.5 months.

2. Enterprise Agent Platform - Agent Governance
Maturity: Proof of concept
Period: 2026
Role: Personal project; designer and builder

Problem: Enterprises can give an agent capability long before they can answer three questions:
who is authorised to approve it, what it may touch, and how anyone proves afterwards what it
did.
Constraint: Workflow engines and AI copilots assume either that a person is present at every
step, or that nothing requires approval. Neither survives contact with a regulated process.
My role: Designed the process model, the governance model and the working prototype.
Mechanisms designed: Named-signer mandate with delegation and separation of duties; six
fail-closed gates bound to artifact version and parameter digest; a dual ledger (business facts
plus hash-chained attestations) with reconciliation; approve, stop and audit as first-class
operations.
Maturity detail: Proof of concept, built in two weeks; zero scenarios released to production.
At capture this was the build phase, not the release phase, and that distinction is the reason
the design exists.
Validation: Discussed and tested with APAC process owners and IT business partners, then
presented to the regional IT VP and the global AI and Data VP as a prototype of AI
transformation.
Verification: 475 processes, 8-step delivery journey, 6 gates, dual ledger; 26 business
scenarios, 6 agent workstreams, 191 EPM processes, 2,073 BPMN agent cells, 103 human roles.

3. Agentic Business Process Management - End-to-End Process Redesign
Maturity: Proof of concept
Period: 2026
Role: Personal project

Problem: AI adoption stalls at the task level because an enterprise governs processes, not
prompts; the unit that needs redesign is the end-to-end process, with agents inside it.
My role: Redesigned five end-to-end process families as agent-hosted flows: Source-to-Settle,
Plan-to-Make, Make-to-Stock, Order-to-Cash and Finance.
Mechanisms designed: Agent-to-process attachment points, human approval points, agent registry
and activation, knowledge and governance modules, so that an agent is an accountable process
participant rather than a plugin.
Maturity detail: Proof of concept; prototype interfaces only.
Verification: 26 scenarios, 6 workstreams and 103 human roles modelled.

4. Enterprise Harness - Open Definition and Reference Implementation
Maturity: Personal prototype, published and open
Period: 2026
Role: Author of the definition, the eleven documents and the reference implementation

Problem: The industry defines what an agent is, but rarely defines what makes its work
acceptable to an enterprise; that layer had no name and no reference implementation.
Mechanisms designed: Agent = Model + Harness, extended to Enterprise Harness = Harness +
Mandate + Ledger; the six-gate ladder; the dual ledger and its reconciliation.
Maturity detail: Published and open. 83 tests, Python 3.11+, no dependencies, so the
specification is falsifiable rather than merely described.
Verification: https://github.com/sdr010-coder/EnterpriseHARNESS
